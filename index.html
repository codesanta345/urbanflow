About us

Urban Flow is an intelligent web-based platform designed to make modern city traffic management more efficient, responsive, and data-driven. The system centralizes traffic monitoring, incident reporting, congestion forecasting, route recommendations, and emergency vehicle prioritization.

By integrating a modern frontend, Express backend APIs, an ML-based prediction pipeline, and graph algorithms (Dijkstra / A*), our mission is to eliminate bottlenecks, cut fuel consumption, and guarantee swift emergency responses.
